What changed

The engine — model.js, health.js, nearby.js, the trained weights — was kept. One line of model.js changed, for the reason in the findings below.

Identity

  • Named Warif — an Arabic word for shade that spreads wide and greenery that is lush.
  • A mark drawn from Al Sadu: a leaf smooth on one side, woven on the other, with the diamond motif at its heart.
  • Reem Kufi and Readex Pro, self-hosted — the venue wifi can drop without the typography going with it.
  • The woven band replaces the flag rule. The flag now appears once, where it means something.

Interface

  • A real desktop layout: the viewfinder is on screen at 1366 px without scrolling.
  • Ten species drawn to their actual leaf structure, replacing three emoji shared between ten trees.
  • Measurements are drawn as countable cells rather than smooth bars.
  • Four themes, each a decision: Auto, Day, Night, High contrast.

Honesty

  • Every count reads the model file: “10 trees in the library, 4 recognised by the current model”.
  • Untrained trees carry a Reference only badge everywhere they appear.
  • The Test Lab renders measurements, never estimates, each with its device and date.
  • Unconfirmed facts render a visible To confirm marker taken from the data.

Showcase kit

  • #/showcase — kiosk: wake lock, rotating facts, resets itself after 90 seconds.
  • #/present — six slides, live figures, a timer that marks the 4:00 floor and 10:00 ceiling. Runs 7:15.
  • #/poster — prints to a single 594 × 841 mm page.
  • #/feedback — four ratings, stored on the device, CSV export at ?admin=1.
  • #/brand — the identity and its rules.

What broke, and what changed

The six that matter. All thirteen are in docs/ITERATIONS.md, and a shorter version is inside the app at #/project/journey.

Fixed

A photograph of a desk came back as a healthy Ghaf

Wood grain returned “Ghaf, 98% — Leaf health 93 out of 100, healthy”, with treatment advice and three shops to buy it from. The app had scored the image at 0.36 similarity against a 0.59 threshold — it knew — but one objection only downgrades an answer to “treat with caution”.

A similarity far below the threshold now refuses on its own. Over 480 photographs of the four trained species exactly one falls below that margin; the non-leaf probes sit at 0.31–0.38. Now: 4 of 4 refused, 0 of 80 real leaves refused.

Fixed

The app claimed to work offline and did not

The service worker was never registering. It was registered from a load listener, but the shell now waits for the model card first — so the listener was attached to an event that had already fired. The app looked healthy and had no offline cache at all.

Found by npm run bench. Now 98 files precached, and both the bench and the test suite reload with the network off.

Fixed

The species count on screen disagreed with the model

“Species: 10” beside a four-class model. Anyone who scanned a mangrove leaf and got “Ghaf” had been told by our own page that mangrove was covered.

Counts now come from metadata.classes.length, and npm test fails if the badges on screen disagree with the model file.

Fixed

Every species drawing rendered as nothing

The leaf shapes emitted their SVG path data as text content rather than inside a <path d>. Ten drawings rendered as ten empty boxes, silently.

The Ghaf and the Mesquite now differ on screen the way the real leaves do, which is the whole argument for drawing them.

Stated, not hidden

The refusal does not catch what we assumed

We believed the unknown-leaf check would refuse a tree the model had never seen. It refuses things that are not plants — and refused 0 of 40 Mesquite.

Stated on the How page and in the Test Lab with its numbers. The demonstration sample that relied on the assumption was removed rather than kept because it happened to work.

Open

The sensitivity suite is failing, and has been left failing

Synthetic chlorosis at 38% of the leaf is detected 89.4% of the time — below the suite's own 90% floor, so npm run sensitivity exits non-zero. It was 93% when the reference set held four species; six of the ten have foliage the thresholds were never tuned against.

Nothing was re-floored and no threshold was loosened to make the red go away. Necrosis is unaffected at 99.2% and 100%.
